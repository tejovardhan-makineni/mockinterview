# Support and feedback

For setup help, search this repository's issues, then open a question describing
your OS, relevant versions, command, expected behavior and redacted error. For a
bug, include reproduction steps and whether it happened in hosted, local or demo
mode. Do not include a provider key, resume or full interview transcript.

For mockinterview.live account access, private data requests or Code of Conduct
reports, email the project maintainer at
[makinenitejovardhan@gmail.com](mailto:makinenitejovardhan@gmail.com). You do not
need to sign in. Include only the details needed to handle your request; never
send passwords, recovery links or API keys. For another installation, contact
its operator, who controls its accounts and data. Operators can set
`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` when building the web app to show their own contact
on the Help & support page.

When signed in, use **Share feedback** beside Practice in the header, or **Report
a problem** in the interview room. Choose a category and add a rating or comment.
A useful report identifies what happened, what you expected and the steps or
moment that reproduce it. Keep the saved reference ID for follow-up. Failed
submissions keep your draft, including when you close and reopen the dialog on
the same page; navigating away or reloading clears an unsent draft.

Feedback records the account, category, rating/comment, entry point, save time
and review status. Feedback from an interview or a loaded report also links the
relevant interview. Page path, browser, viewport, language, time zone and
connection details are included only when you enable diagnostics; transcript
sharing is a separate opt-in. Share only information you consent to provide.
Feedback is for improving the service and is separate from the interview
assessment. Demo mode does not submit or store feedback.

Propose a format using the format issue template, or contribute through
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). For vulnerabilities, use the private process
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This is a community preview with no guaranteed
support response time or availability commitment.

## Community and local setup

Follow [the local setup guide](docs/LOCAL-SETUP.md) for macOS, Windows and Linux,
including the Docker-free demo and persistent database setup.
Join [Discord](https://discord.gg/KvGunFKZwS) for conversation,
[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/mockinterview_live/) for practice stories, or
[GitHub](https://github.com/tejovardhan-makineni/mockinterview) for contributions.
Keep private records out of all public community channels.

Request a new template through `/requests`, or apply at `/beta` for administrator
review. Approved beta testers agree to share feedback. Optional **Share analytics**
for local apps is separate from ordinary local history; it needs a configured
remote API, sign-in and consent. Failed uploads do not interrupt practice and
are not a guaranteed backup. Private feedback is not published as a testimonial
without separate permission.
