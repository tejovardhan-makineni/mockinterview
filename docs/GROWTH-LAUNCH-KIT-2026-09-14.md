## Demonstration script: about 60 seconds

- **0–5 seconds:** “Here is a design decision I want to get better at explaining.” Show an original fictional project prompt.
- **5–25 seconds:** Show an actual answer and follow-up from the product. Choose a moment that clearly depends on the answer. Edit for time without changing its meaning.
- **25–45 seconds:** Show the saved report and the answer evidence supporting one comment. Explain the next practice step.
- **45–60 seconds:** “This is mockinterview.live. It's an open-source public beta. Try a practice interview and tell me which follow-ups or feedback you'd change.” Show the URL, 18+ audience, and current weekly allowance.

Use the founder's own fictional practice, or separately approved participant material. Label reenactments and sample reports as examples. Never manufacture a successful session or a user endorsement.
